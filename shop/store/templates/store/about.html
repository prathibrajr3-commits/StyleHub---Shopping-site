Who We Are

Clothing built on honesty

StyleHub was founded in 2021 with a single belief: great clothing shouldn't cost a fortune, and affordable clothing shouldn't fall apart after three washes. We set out to close that gap.

We work directly with manufacturers, cutting out middlemen to bring you premium-quality cotton shirts, well-tailored trousers, and everyday tees at prices that actually make sense. Every piece in our collection is tested by our in-house team before it earns a spot on the site.

Based in Mumbai, we ship across India with free delivery on orders above ₹500 and a no-questions-asked 7-day return policy.

What We Stand For

Sustainability

We use GOTS-certified organic cotton wherever possible and are actively transitioning all packaging to recycled materials by 2026.

Fair Trade

Every factory we partner with is audited annually for fair wages, safe working conditions, and ethical labour practices.

Quality First

We test every product for colourfastness, shrinkage, and stitching durability before it enters our catalogue. No exceptions.

Customer Love

Real humans answer our support queries within 4 hours. We genuinely care about your experience — before and after purchase.
